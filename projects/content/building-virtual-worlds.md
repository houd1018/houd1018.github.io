[Building Virtual Worlds (Fall 2023)](https://www.etc.cmu.edu/learn/curriculum/building-virtual-worlds/) at Carnegie Mellon University – Entertainment Technology Center.

- Rapid prototyping course requiring five immersive user experiences in Unity using C#
- Collaborated with artists and sound designers to iteratively design games for platforms such as Oculus Quest 2, DDR dance mat and Arduino
- Implemented VFX in Unity using ShaderLab, VFX Graph and particle systems, including vertex manipulation, post-processing, glass and hologram effects, and material interactions
